# 90-Second Demo Script — NEXUS

## 0:00–0:08 — Problem

**Show:** NEXUS landing screen.

**Say:** “At a networking mixer, the problem isn't a lack of people. It's not knowing who is actually here, who is relevant, and why you should approach them.”

## 0:08–0:22 — Enter + live room

**Show:** Enter live demo → choose Aisha Mehta → Home.

**Say:** “NEXUS turns the room into a live, searchable network. The home screen immediately tells me how many people are here, how many connections I've saved, and my strongest next conversation.”

## 0:22–0:43 — Discovery

**Show:** Discover → ranked cards → scroll to Why this match → Details.

**Say:** “This is the key difference from a directory. NEXUS ranks people using shared interests and two-way complementarity between what I can offer and what they are looking for. It also explains the score in plain language, so I know what conversation to start.”

## 0:43–0:55 — Connection memory

**Show:** Connect → My network.

**Say:** “When I find someone valuable, I save the connection. That creates a follow-up queue instead of relying on memory after the event.”

## 0:55–1:10 — Live presence

**Show:** Organizer → Simulate arrival / departure.

**Say:** “The live layer solves the attendance problem too. Check-in and check-out state updates the attendee pool, so Discover stays focused on people who are actually in the room.”

## 1:10–1:22 — Organizer utility

**Show:** Organizer KPIs → Live roster → room composition → Export CSV.

**Say:** “Organizers get the same signal from the other side: live attendance, room composition, saved connections, and an export for follow-up.”

## 1:22–1:30 — Close

**Show:** Home → top match → NEXUS logo.

**Say:** “NEXUS changes networking from ‘who should I talk to?’ into ‘here are the next few people, and here is why.’”

## Recording note

Use a second browser tab during the demo to show the cross-tab live sync. Keep the cursor visible. Zoom the browser to 90–100% and record at 1080p if possible.
